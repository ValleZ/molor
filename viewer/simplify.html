Formula simplifier

Paste a formula. This finds the smallest blend of materials whose predicted character is closest to it. Closeness is reported against what a random blend of the same size achieves and against the nearest single material, because a distance on its own means nothing: a long formula sits in a crowded part of the space where everything looks close. It runs entirely in this page — nothing is uploaded.

Doses are odour-activity corrected.

What it read

What the formula is, by lift

Standardised against 2000 random blends of the same size, because the raw values are dominated by how common each word is in the training labels. A random ten-material blend already scores floral 0.90.

What it loses

Descriptor by descriptor, how the simplification differs from the formula. Everything here is well under the difference between two materials you would struggle to tell apart, so read it as which way the character drifts rather than as how far.

What nothing here can tell you

    ← The receptor atlas

    The predicted character comes from a fingerprint model trained on the GS-LF descriptor set; it reads a blend's bulk and is blind to traces, which is the failure that recurs most in this project. Doses derived from detection thresholds use a compilation from published and web sources, some converted from air-phase figures, which may be out by a factor of ten. For the materials marked measured that compilation has been corrected by a blotter session: each was prepared at its own maximum use level and judged against the others, so what is measured is how much smell it delivers per gram rather than a detection threshold.
